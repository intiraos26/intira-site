Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

INTIRA is a productivity and personal finance app designed to help you manage tasks, track debt payoff, and focus on what matters. Your privacy is fundamental to how INTIRA is built. In short: everything you write stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account, and the only information that reaches us is anonymous, aggregated usage counts that contain none of your content.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account. INTIRA stores your tasks, sprints, blocks, debts, expenses, focus sessions, usage counters, and settings locally on your iPhone. If you are signed into iCloud, this data is also synced to your personal, private iCloud account so it stays up to date across your devices and can be restored if you reinstall the app or set up a new device. INTIRA Labs does not operate its own servers, and the content you enter is never sent to us. The one exception to "nothing leaves your device" is the anonymous usage measurement described under Analytics below, which contains no task, note, debt, or transcript content of any kind.

Cloud Sync (iCloud)

INTIRA uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data through your private iCloud database. Your data is stored in your own iCloud account, tied to your Apple ID — not on servers operated by INTIRA Labs. INTIRA Labs cannot access, read, or view the contents of your private iCloud data; only you, through your signed-in devices, can access it. Apple stores and processes this data as part of iCloud, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Sync is automatic when you are signed into iCloud; if you are not signed in, INTIRA works fully offline using on-device storage only.

Usage Counters On Your Device

So that scoring stays relevant to how you actually work, INTIRA keeps a small set of counters on your device: how many tasks you have completed in each category, roughly how long those tasks took, which days of the week you tend to complete them on, and how many sprints you have finished. The Signal Engine reads these counters as one of the inputs when it calculates a task's score. They are plain counts and timings — no task titles, notes, or other content is included. Like the rest of your data, these counters stay on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, in your private iCloud account. They are never sent to INTIRA Labs or to any third party.

Analytics

INTIRA uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used — for example how many tasks are created, how often sprints are completed, and how many people begin a trial. A fixed, limited set of events is recorded, such as completing onboarding, creating or completing a task, starting or completing a sprint, using voice capture, logging a debt payment, and viewing or completing a purchase.

These events never include your content. No task titles, notes, block text, debt names, balances, expense amounts, or voice transcripts are collected. The only details attached to an event are counts and fixed categories — for example whether a task was classified as Signal or Noise, how many tasks were in a completed sprint, or which subscription product was purchased.

Firebase also automatically records standard technical information, including an app instance identifier, device model, operating system and app version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. Google processes this information as our service provider, subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's data processing terms. INTIRA does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not display ads, does not sell or share this data, and does not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Apple also provides INTIRA Labs with anonymous, aggregated App Analytics through App Store Connect when you have opted in at the device level. You can adjust that under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or account information — INTIRA does not require an account. We do not collect task titles, descriptions, or notes. We do not collect debt names, balances, expense amounts, or any other financial information. We do not collect voice recordings or speech transcripts. We do not track your location. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell or share your personal data. Your content syncs only to your own private iCloud account; the only data that reaches us is the anonymous usage measurement described under "Analytics" above.

Speech Recognition

INTIRA offers an optional voice capture feature that uses Apple's Speech framework to convert speech to text. Where your device supports on-device recognition, audio is processed entirely on your iPhone and never leaves it. Where on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple may process the audio on their servers, subject to Apple's own privacy policy. INTIRA does not store, transmit, or retain any audio recordings, and the resulting text is saved only as the task you choose to keep.

Microphone Access

INTIRA requests microphone access solely for the voice capture feature. The microphone is only active while you are recording, and stops as soon as you tap to stop. Audio is used exclusively for real-time speech-to-text conversion and is not recorded or stored.

Calendar Access

INTIRA includes an optional "Sync to Calendar" setting. When you turn it on, iOS will ask for permission to access your calendars, and INTIRA will add tasks that have a due date to your default calendar so they appear alongside the rest of your schedule. Only the task title and its date are written, and INTIRA keeps the resulting event identifier on your device so it can update or remove that event when the task changes or is deleted. INTIRA does not read your existing calendar events for any other purpose, and no calendar information is transmitted to INTIRA Labs or any third party. You can revoke access at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Notifications

INTIRA can send reminders for tasks and sprints. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device — INTIRA does not use push notification servers and does not send your device token or task content to any server.

In-App Purchases

INTIRA offers optional Pro subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. INTIRA does not collect or store payment information, credit card numbers, or Apple ID credentials. We record only that a purchase, restore, or trial start occurred, as described under "Analytics."

Widgets and Live Activities

INTIRA may display task and sprint information on your Home Screen via widgets and Lock Screen via Live Activities. This data is read from a shared on-device container and is never transmitted externally.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by using the "Factory Reset" option in Settings. This permanently removes all tasks, sprints, blocks, debts, expenses, focus history, usage counters, and settings. Because your data syncs to your private iCloud account, note that uninstalling the app does not by itself erase your iCloud copy — that copy remains so your data can be restored when you reinstall or set up a new device. To remove the iCloud copy as well, use "Factory Reset" before uninstalling, or remove INTIRA's data from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.

Children's Privacy

INTIRA does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation, and the content you enter syncs only to your own private iCloud account — never to servers operated by INTIRA Labs. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Because INTIRA does not hold your content, most requests to access, correct, or delete your data are handled entirely on your device using "Factory Reset." If you are in a region with additional data protection rights — such as the EEA, the UK, or California — and you have questions about the anonymous usage data described above, contact us at privacy@intira.io and we will respond.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of INTIRA after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at privacy@intira.io, or reach the team at support@intira.io.
