THE SIGNAL ENGINE

Know what matters before you act.

INTIRA doesn't just hold your list. It scores and sorts each task using deadlines, priority, language, and context — surfacing Signal when it deserves attention and moving everything else to Noise until it matters.

The result is a list that stays useful instead of becoming another thing to manage.

iCloud Backup & Restore

Your tasks, debts, blocks, and progress are backed up to your private iCloud — so nothing is lost when you reinstall or set up a new device. Backup and restore are free, stored privately in your own account, never on our servers. Pro adds sync across multiple devices. Not signed into iCloud? INTIRA works fully offline on your device.

WHY INTIRA

Built to answer one question: Where do I start?

I built INTIRA because I wanted a system that could look at everything on my plate and quietly show me where to start. Not another list that grows until it's overwhelming, but something that reasons about what matters and lets me act with intention. Add the task, let the Signal Engine sort it, and trust that the top of the list is the right place to begin.

That's the whole philosophy, in one word: intention. Move through your days guided by a little intelligence — and spend your energy on action, not organization.

— Eve Hyppolite, Founder, INTIRA Labs Why INTIRA →
