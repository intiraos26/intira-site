Focus on Signal. Cut the Noise.

Attention is the scarce resource. The question isn't how much you can get through — it's what deserves you in the first place.

SIGNAL

  • Work that moves an outcome forward
  • Decisions that actually matter
  • Information that changes what happens next
  • Priorities aligned with intent

NOISE

  • Activity without meaningful progress
  • Competing, redundant information
  • Low-value demands on attention
  • Things that feel urgent but aren't important
INTIRA
OUR FIRST PRODUCT

The philosophy, made practical.

INTIRA is the first product from INTIRA Labs. It's a productivity and focus system designed to reduce the cognitive burden of deciding what deserves attention.

Capture what is competing for your attention, separate signal from noise, then move through focused execution with Brain Dump, the Signal Engine, Sprints, Focus Mode, Blocks, Voice Capture, and Snowball.

The Signal Engine scores and sorts tasks using deterministic, inspectable rules.

Eve Hyppolite is a product leader, storyteller, speaker, and multidisciplinary creator whose work sits at the intersection of technology, human behavior, communication, and experience.

What if technology helped us think more clearly instead of simply giving us more to manage?
